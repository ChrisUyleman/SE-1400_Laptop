A history of Sunbros

Sunbros started as a way to give back to the community of Sunbrook, where old retirement communities often have trouble doing manual labor. At its core, Sunbros was just two friends who banded together to help the community and each other. Eventually, they decided to start a business to grow their reach and help those even outside of Sunbrook.

Despite this growth, Sunbros kept its name as a reminder of where we came from, and the values we want to uphold. No matter where in St. George you might be located, we will always stay true to the roots we have attained over the years.

This includes free quotes, warranties, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee. We want to make sure that you are happy with the work we do, and if you aren't, we will do everything in our power to make it right.

We are always looking for ways to improve our services, so if you have any suggestions, please let us know!
